About Ekam

Our Story

Curation, not just coordination.

Ekam is a boutique event planning and décor brand built on the idea of oneness, harmony, and meaningful storytelling. Born from the founder's own wedding experience, Ekam was created to transform stressful, fragmented event planning into calm, thoughtful, beautifully coordinated celebrations.

At Ekam, events are not treated as transactions but as chapters in a person's life story. Every detail is carefully considered so each celebration feels personal, balanced, and deeply meaningful.

Kavya Neelakanta
Founder
Vision & Mission

Vision

To establish Ekam as a trusted boutique event brand in Visakhapatnam, known for its warmth, creativity and soulful storytelling — expanding across South India over time.

Mission

To create calm, meaningful, beautifully managed celebrations by blending thoughtful planning, refined aesthetics and genuine care — delivering experiences that feel effortless, personal and memorable.
